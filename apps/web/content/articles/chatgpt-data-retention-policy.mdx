ChatGPT's retention policy depends on your workspace, the feature you use, and whether you delete the data. Turning off model training does not delete saved chats. A business plan's no-training default does not mean nothing is stored.

The broad preservation order from the 2025 copyright litigation ended on September 26, 2025, according to OpenAI. Limited historical data was still subject to preservation in its October 2025 update. That is different from an ongoing requirement to keep every new conversation indefinitely.

Sources checked October 28, 2026. The sections below separate current product policies from that dated litigation update.

<h2 id="what-chatgpt-stores-data-by-default">What ChatGPT stores by default</h2>

Regular and archived chats stay saved until you delete them or an applicable workspace policy removes them. Archiving is not deletion. When you delete a chat, it disappears from your account immediately and is scheduled for permanent deletion within 30 days. OpenAI lists exceptions for content already de-identified and disassociated from your account, and for security or legal obligations. This is a deletion window, not a guarantee that every chat is held for exactly 30 days. See [chat and file retention](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8983778-chat-and-file-retention-in-chatgpt).

Files and memories need separate attention. Deleting a chat does not delete a file saved in Library; files in a custom GPT or project follow their own lifecycle. A saved memory can also survive deletion of the chat that created it. Review the [Memory controls](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8590148-memory-in-chatgpt) and remove the information from its relevant sources, including chats, saved memories, Library files, or connected apps.

<h2 id="the-court-order-you-probably-missed">What happened to the 2025 court order?</h2>

OpenAI's [October 22, 2025 update](https://openai.com/index/response-to-nyt-data-demands/) says the earlier obligation to preserve consumer ChatGPT and API content indefinitely ended on **September 26, 2025**. It resumed standard retention practices for new data.

That update describes a limited historical April–September 2025 data set kept separately for legal obligations, with access restricted to an audited legal and security team. It also says conversations originating in the EEA, Switzerland, and UK were no longer subject to that preservation requirement. ChatGPT Enterprise, Edu, and qualifying ZDR API usage had been excluded from the earlier order.

This is OpenAI's dated account of the preservation order, not a claim that every later discovery dispute is resolved or that no future legal hold can apply. Check current legal and contractual requirements when evaluating sensitive records.

## The "Opt Out of Training" Setting and What It Actually Does

In personal Free, Plus, and Pro workspaces, model-improvement sharing is enabled by default. Open **Settings → Data controls → Improve the model for everyone** and turn it off to exclude new conversations. You can keep chat history while opting out. [OpenAI's data controls guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/7730893-data-controls-in-chatgpt) explains the available settings.

The opt-out applies to new conversations; it is not a promise to reverse training already completed. If you voluntarily submit response feedback, the associated conversation may still be used for training, even after opting out. Business workspace feedback has different rules. See [how data is used to improve models](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/5722486-how-your-data-is-used-to-improve-model-performance).

Temporary chats are not used for training while they remain temporary, and OpenAI may retain a copy for up to 30 days for safety. Saving a temporary chat turns it into a regular chat governed by your account's settings. Training, personalization, and retention are separate controls.

<h3 id="1-free-plus-pro-team">1. Personal plans and ChatGPT Business (formerly Team)</h3>

Personal plans have the training choice described above. **ChatGPT Business is different:** OpenAI does not train on business workspace inputs and outputs by default. [Team was renamed Business in August 2025](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8542115-chatgpt-business-general-faq); it should not be grouped with consumer training defaults.

OpenAI's [business retention FAQ](https://openai.com/enterprise-privacy/) describes workspace admin retention controls and deletion within 30 days, subject to legal or harm-prevention exceptions. Confirm your workspace's actual policy. A no-training default is not an API ZDR agreement.

### 2. ChatGPT Enterprise and Edu

These workspaces also exclude business data from training by default. Admins control retention; OpenAI says deleted conversations are removed within 30 days unless legal obligations require otherwise. The [enterprise privacy documentation](https://openai.com/enterprise-privacy/) covers access and retention controls. Review your organization's policy before assuming a saved conversation expires automatically.

### 3. API (Standard)

API data is not used for training by default. Abuse-monitoring logs generally last up to 30 days, with legal and safety exceptions. Application state is separate: some features retain content until deletion. See the [API retention table](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data).

### 4. API (Zero Data Retention)

ZDR requires approval and excludes customer content from abuse-monitoring logs. It forces `store=false` for Responses and Chat Completions, but ineligible endpoints can still store application state. Model, caching, image/file safety, and notified retention exceptions also apply. Check the [current endpoint limitations](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/your-data) for your exact request.

For organizations using [ZDR with Private Safety Processing](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/private-safety-processing), customer-controlled storage and additional configuration are required. Selecting a model or adding an API key does not establish an approved ZDR configuration.

## What About GDPR and HIPAA?

For PHI, verify an executed BAA, an eligible product, and its covered functionality before processing patient information. OpenAI's [current eligibility list](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001069-hipaa-eligible-products-and-functionality) includes specific healthcare and regulated workspaces and API configurations; coverage does not extend automatically to every feature or connected service. The [API BAA guide](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8660679-getting-a-business-associate-agreement-for-the-openai-api) explains eligibility and enrollment. Self-serve ChatGPT Business is not a substitute for a contracted offering with a BAA, as its [plan overview](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/8792828-chatgpt-business-overview) explains.

For organizational personal-data processing, review OpenAI's [DPA](https://openai.com/policies/data-processing-addendum/), your agreement, and the applicable transfer safeguards. A DPA supports compliance; it does not settle your lawful basis, disclosure, access, or deletion obligations. OpenAI also offers [regional storage and processing options for eligible customers](https://openai.com/business-data/). Confirm product, region, and endpoint coverage instead of assuming every request is processed in the US or that regional storage covers every operation.

## So What's the Practical Risk?

Before sending a transcript or document, answer three questions:

- **Where will it be stored?** Include saved chats, memories, files, application state, and any connected destination.
- **Can it be used for training?** Check the workspace and opt-in settings rather than inferring the answer from price or model name.
- **Who can access it, and what exceptions apply?** Review your organization's permissions, agreement, and relevant safety or legal requirements.

These choices matter even when training is disabled. For meeting-specific consent and handling questions, continue with the [AI note-taking legal guide](/blog/is-ai-notetaking-legal/).

## The OpenAI API Has Meaningfully Better Data Controls

The API offers organization and project retention controls for approved customers. To evaluate a tool, identify its actual endpoint, enabled features, storage behavior, and third-party destinations before relying on a retention setting.
