Claude does not have one data-retention policy for every product. Consumer chats, commercial workspaces, API requests, feedback, and particular models have different rules.

**Sources checked October 28, 2026.** Start with the product and account you use, then check training permissions, deletion, model restrictions, and enabled features. A local copy of your notes does not prevent a hosted AI provider from receiving a summary request.

## What Does Claude Store by Default?

For Claude Free, Pro, and Max, saved conversations remain in your account until you delete them. Anthropic's [consumer retention guide](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/10023548-how-long-do-you-store-my-data) says deletion removes a chat from your history immediately and from backend storage within 30 days. That is a deletion window, not automatic expiry of every saved chat after 30 days.

Other cases have different clocks:

- **Model improvement enabled:** New or resumed chats can remain in de-identified training pipelines for up to five years.
- **Safety flags:** Inputs and outputs can be retained for up to two years, with classification scores retained for up to seven years.
- **Feedback and bug reports:** Associated data can be kept for five years.
- **Incognito:** Chats are excluded from model improvement; that does not make the session local or prove immediate deletion.

Legal obligations and usage-policy enforcement can extend retention. Check the relevant exception before treating a headline number as a guarantee.

## The September 2025 Training Policy Change

Anthropic's [August 28, 2025 announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/updates-to-our-consumer-terms) introduced a consumer choice about model improvement. Its current text gives existing users until October 8, 2025 to choose, after which a selection was required to continue using Claude. It does not say that missing September 28 silently enrolled everyone's chats.

The change applies to consumer Free, Pro, and Max accounts, including Claude Code used with those accounts. Commercial services were excluded. Enabling the setting applies the longer training retention to new or resumed conversations; inactive older chats are not automatically swept in.

Training choice and saved-chat deletion are separate controls. Declining model improvement does not erase conversations you keep in your account.

## How to Check and Change Your Settings

Open **Settings → Privacy** in Claude. Anthropic's [current settings guide](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/12109829-how-do-i-change-my-model-improvement-privacy-settings) calls the control **Help Improve our AI models**.

Turning it off excludes both previously stored and new chats or coding sessions from future model-training runs. It does not undo training already underway or models already trained. Safety-flagged conversations may still support internal safeguards work.

[Consumer training rules](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/10023580-is-my-data-used-for-model-training) also distinguish ordinary model-improvement permission from feedback, safety review, and explicit testing programs. A disabled toggle is not a promise that no data can be reviewed or used for any safety purpose.

## How Is the Anthropic API Different for Privacy-Conscious Users?

*This diagram summarizes the ordinary training choice and API path. The API's no-training default has feedback/permission exceptions; retention also depends on the model, feature, and agreement below.*

Anthropic's [commercial retention policy](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996866-how-long-do-you-store-my-organization-s-data) gives standard API inputs and outputs a backend deletion window of up to 30 days. Exceptions include storage-dependent services such as the Files API, different contractual arrangements, policy enforcement, and legal requirements. Saved commercial chats remain available until deleted; they do not all expire after one API-style window.

Under an approved ZDR arrangement, eligible API prompts and responses are not stored at rest after the response. [API eligibility documentation](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/api-and-data-retention) lists feature-specific limits: stateful features can retain data outside ZDR, and flagged content or legal holds can require retention. A non-Covered model alone does not guarantee zero retention for every feature.

The current [Covered Models list](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15425695-covered-models) includes Fable 5, Fable 5.1, Mythos 5, and Mythos 5.1. Their default minimum is 30 days, subject to safety/legal exceptions. Anthropic documents a limited transitional ZDR option for eligible Fable business use with explicit authorization; do not assume it applies to your account. Other models follow your existing agreement and settings.

## Claude for Work, Enterprise, Edu, and Gov

Commercial products have a **no-training default**, not an unconditional never-training promise. Anthropic's [commercial training policy](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996868-is-my-data-used-for-model-training) allows training when customers explicitly permit it or submit feedback. Feedback can include the related conversation and remain for up to five years. Team and Enterprise owners can disable the chat-rating feedback control.

Review your contract and organizational controls before submitting confidential meeting material. Training permission, retention, and who can access a saved workspace are different questions.

## What About HIPAA and GDPR?

**HIPAA:** A signed BAA and the correct HIPAA-ready configuration are prerequisites, not a blanket compliance guarantee for every Claude surface or connected tool. The [current BAA matrix](https://support.claude.com/en/articles/15455031-covered-models-under-a-business-associate-agreement-baa) includes eligible API and Enterprise Chat configurations, plus certain Enterprise Claude Code and Cowork local modes with the required HIPAA configuration. It does not cover every Code/Cowork setup.

The [API feature table](https://platform.claude.com/docs/en/manage-claude/api-and-data-retention) marks web search eligible but excludes its dynamic-filtering feature; consumer plans are outside API HIPAA readiness. Verify the exact product, model, feature, and contract before sending PHI. Connecting a key through another application does not automatically bring that application under Anthropic's BAA.

**GDPR:** Anthropic's [DPA guide](https://privacy.claude.com/en/articles/7996862-how-do-i-view-and-sign-your-data-processing-addendum-dpa) says its DPA and Standard Contractual Clauses are incorporated into its Commercial Terms. Access through a third-party service can involve that service's terms. A DPA does not by itself settle your organization's lawful basis, recording permissions, or retention policy; use the [meeting consent guide](/blog/is-ai-notetaking-legal/) to identify questions for your legal team.

## Claude's Reddit Lawsuit Worth Knowing About

Reddit's [June 4, 2025 complaint](https://redditinc.com/hubfs/Reddit%20Inc/Content/PDFs/Docket%20Stamped%20Complaint.pdf) alleges unauthorized use of Reddit content, including contractual and privacy-related concerns. These are allegations, not findings established by the complaint.

That training-data sourcing dispute is separate from the policies governing a meeting transcript you submit through Claude or its API. This guide does not establish the case's current procedural status; evaluate your own data path using the product policies and agreement above.
