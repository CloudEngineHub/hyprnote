Mistral is headquartered in France. That does not establish where every API request is processed, how long every product stores content, or whether your account excludes data from training.

Vibe, formerly Le Chat, and Mistral Studio expose different controls. Start with the product, endpoint, and account settings you actually use.

Sources checked October 30, 2026. Where Mistral's help articles and developer documentation differ, those limits are identified below.

## What Mistral Stores by Default

![Mistral's default training status across Vibe and Studio, by plan](anarlog/blog/articles/mistral-data-retention-policy/mistral-training-defaults.png)

*Training preferences only. This figure does not describe storage duration, processing location, or the separate handling of submitted feedback.*

Mistral splits data handling into two products with separate controls: Vibe, the chat and agent product formerly called Le Chat, and Mistral Studio, the developer platform and API. Opting out of one does not opt you out of the other.

The [privacy policy](https://legal.mistral.ai/terms/privacy-policy/) describes 30 rolling days of API input/output retention for abuse monitoring, unless ZDR is activated, with exceptions for specific APIs. It describes different schedules for Vibe history, Agents, and fine-tuning data, and allows longer retention for legal obligations or proceedings. This is not a universal deletion deadline.

Its scope also matters: the privacy policy distinguishes Mistral acting as controller from processing personal data on a business customer's behalf. For business processing, review the applicable agreement and [Data Processing Addendum](https://legal.mistral.ai/terms/data-processing-addendum/).

Account metadata has separate legal retention periods. Mistral's [retention help article](https://help.mistral.ai/en/articles/347628-how-long-do-you-store-my-data) lists five years for civil identity after the user contract ends, one year for other account data after deletion, and one rolling year for technical connection data. These are examples, not the complete schedule for every data category.

## Where Your Data Is Stored

Mistral's [August hosting help article](https://help.mistral.ai/en/articles/347629-where-do-you-store-my-data-or-my-organization-s-data) says EU hosting is the default, while acknowledging feature-dependent transfers outside the EU. Its current [regional inference documentation](https://docs.mistral.ai/inference/regional-inference) makes a narrower, explicit distinction:

- `api.mistral.ai` is global and carries no commitment to a specific inference location.
- `api.eu.mistral.ai` and `api.us.mistral.ai` select regional inference. The docs describe the EU endpoint's geography as EU and EFTA data centers.
- Regional inference adds 10% to standard token pricing and has model and feature restrictions. It does not make account, billing, API-key, or other control-plane data regional.

Do not treat the global endpoint as an EU processing guarantee. Verify the chosen endpoint, supported workload, and contractual geography requirements. Regional inference does not establish where every associated service stores data.

## Is Zero Data Retention Applicable?

The [ZDR developer guide](https://docs.mistral.ai/admin/monitor-comply/zero-data-retention) covers supported stateless endpoints such as chat completions, embeddings, and OCR. It excludes Labs models and stateful products including Agents, Conversations, Libraries, Files, batch files, Vibe Work, and Chat. A local CLI can still call a hosted API; coverage follows the underlying request.

Plan wording differs: the [Help Center](https://help.mistral.ai/en/articles/347612-can-i-activate-zero-data-retention-zdr) describes pay-as-you-go eligibility; the developer guide says paid plans. Both require a reviewed request. Confirm approval for your Organization in Admin privacy settings and verify the model and endpoint before relying on ZDR. A paid subscription or training opt-out alone is not proof that it is active.

## Training Opt-Out in Plain Terms

Mistral's [training-controls article](https://help.mistral.ai/en/articles/347617-do-you-use-my-user-data-to-train-your-artificial-intelligence-models) and [opt-out instructions](https://help.mistral.ai/en/articles/455207-can-i-opt-out-of-my-input-or-output-data-being-used-for-training) distinguish these settings:

- **Vibe Free, Pro, Team**: opted in to training by default. Opt out individually in Settings, or organization-wide if you're a Team admin.
- **Vibe Enterprise**: opted out of training by default. An admin manages the opt-in toggle.
- **Studio Free mode**: opted in to training by default. Opt out in the Admin Panel.
- **Studio pay-as-you-go**: has its own opt-out toggle in the Admin Panel, under Privacy, then Anonymous improvement data. Mistral does not publish this tier as excluded by default, so check your organization's setting rather than assume it.

Vibe and API controls are separate. The training article also treats submitted feedback, including the associated input and output, separately from the normal training preference. Avoid sending confidential content as feedback if that use is not approved.

## GDPR and Compliance

Mistral publishes a DPA for business processing and a privacy policy describing individual rights and request channels. Review the agreement, service scope, subprocessors, transfers, and deletion terms that apply to your use. Company headquarters alone does not settle that assessment.

For a regulated workflow, obtain confirmation of the required contracts and covered services from Mistral. Neither a DPA, a training toggle, nor a regional endpoint by itself establishes suitability for your data.

## How Mistral Compares With Other AI Providers

Compare equivalent products and configurations. A chat subscription, a stateless API request, and a stored agent conversation can have different policies within the same vendor. Check processing region, retention, training use, stateful features, and contract coverage separately.

Our [OpenAI](/blog/chatgpt-data-retention-policy/) and [Anthropic](/blog/anthropic-data-retention-policy/) guides explain those products' controls. Do not infer another vendor's business-plan defaults from Mistral's Vibe settings.
